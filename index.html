Colleagues keep messaging you: “Can I use this?” Ask them what you need to know, then rule with the three checks and tell them what to do.

1The taskIs this kind of task covered by the AI Usage Policy?
2The toolIs the tool one the policy lists?
3The dataWhich class goes in, and may this tool take it?

    The decision model in your pocket

    1. The task: prohibited, stop. Silent, ask InfoSec. Permitted, go on to check 2.
    2. The tool: on the policy’s list, go on to check 3. New, vet it, ask InfoSec and wait.
    3. The data: name the class and match it to the tool’s tier. If it doesn’t fit, anonymize first or pick another tool.
